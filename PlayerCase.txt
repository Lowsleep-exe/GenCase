---

**CASE FILE** has been established for the double murder committed in Central Park, New York City, on September 14, 2023, at 10:37 PM. The victim, Ms. Emily Chen, a 32-year-old software developer, and her colleague, Mr. Michael Lee, a 34-year-old financial analyst, were found dead in the park. Detective Johnathan Harper, the lead investigator, is in the process of gathering evidence and interviewing suspects to solve this mysterious crime.

---

### INCIDENT REPORT

At approximately 10:37 PM, park security officer, Mr. David Kim, discovered Ms. Chen and Mr. Lee's bodies in Central Park. Both victims were found in the center of the park, in close proximity, with their hands tied behind their backs. The bodies were discovered by a surveillance camera, which captured their last movements. The scene showed no signs of forced entry, indicating a premeditated attack. Upon investigation, the scene was left untouched, suggesting the perpetrators had taken precautions to avoid detection. No forced entry points were found at the scene. 

---

### CRIME SCENE REPORT

The crime scene is a well-lit area with a full moon illuminating the surroundings. The bodies of Ms. Emily Chen and Mr. Michael Lee were found lying in a shallow depression between two oak trees. Both individuals had their hands bound with a rope, and both had stab wounds to the chest. The rope used was identified as a standard cotton rope, not matching any ropes in the park. The bodies were facing each other, forming a makeshift casket. There was no sign of forced entry, no signs of forced entry into nearby buildings, and no signs of forced exit from the park. 

The bodies were surrounded by scattered leaves and branches, indicating the area was disturbed shortly after the murders. Footprints were left in the mud, leading towards a nearby tennis court. A security camera captured these footprints, and the prints matched one of the suspects. Bloodstains were found on the floor, and forensic technicians are examining these for identification.
